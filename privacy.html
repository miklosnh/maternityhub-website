This Privacy Policy is designed to comply with global privacy standards, including the European Union General Data Protection Regulation ("EU GDPR"), the UK General Data Protection Regulation ("UK GDPR") and Data Protection Act 2018, the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection ("FADP"), the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act ("CCPA/CPRA"), the Washington My Health My Data Act ("MHMDA"), Nevada Revised Statutes Chapter 603A, Canada’s Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act ("PIPEDA"), and the Australian Privacy Principles ("APPs") under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

Our Core Privacy Promise:
Your body, your family, and your data belong to you. We do not sell, rent, trade, or give away your personal information or health data to third parties. We never use your fertility, cycle, pregnancy, or infant care logs for third-party advertising, behavioral profiling, or data brokerage. Your information is processed strictly to provide, synchronize, secure, and improve the Services you request.

2. Information We Collect

We adhere to the principle of data minimization and collect only the information necessary to deliver the core functionality of the App across its Trying to Conceive (TTC), Pregnancy, and Infant Care chapters.

2.1. Personal Information You Provide Directly

  • Account & Profile Information: When you create an account, we collect your email address, name or preferred nickname, encrypted password, and basic profile settings (such as your selected App chapter/mode, preferred measurement units, language, and time zone). Providing a first name or nickname is optional.
  • Partner & Family Sharing Information: If you choose to invite a partner, spouse, or family caregiver to synchronize logs, we collect the invited member’s email address or link their authenticated account to your shared family profile at your explicit direction.
  • Customer Support Communications: When you contact us at support@intervadasolutions.com or contact@intervadasolutions.com, we collect your name, email address, and the contents of your message so we can assist you.

2.2. Sensitive Health, Maternal, and Infant Care Data You Voluntarily Log

Depending on which chapters and features of the App you choose to use, you may voluntarily enter health-related and developmental data ("Special Category Data" under the EU/UK GDPR and "Consumer Health Data" / "Sensitive Personal Information" under applicable U.S., Canadian, and Australian laws): 1. Trying to Conceive (TTC) & Cycle Tracking Data: Menstrual period start and end dates, menstrual flow intensity, basal body temperature (BBT), cervical mucus observations, ovulation test results, pregnancy test results, relevant fertility-related medical conditions you choose to note, medications/pills/supplements taken, physical symptoms, mood, and daily water/hydration intake. 2. Pregnancy Data: Estimated due date, last menstrual period or conception date, maternal weight logs, fetal movement/kick counts, physical activity, rest and sleep logs, appetite, pregnancy symptoms, mood, hydration, and prenatal vitamins/medications taken. 3. Infant Sleep & Daily Care Data: Your child’s name or nickname, birth date/age, wake times, nap start and end times, bedtimes, night wakings, breastfeeding sessions (duration and side), bottle feeding volumes and contents, breast milk pumping logs, diaper changes (wet/soiled), baby body temperature, baby medication logs, and baby bath routines. 4. Infant Solids & Nutrition Data: Solid foods introduced across food groups (vegetables, fruits, proteins, grains, dairy, healthy fats, beans, seeds, herbs), water intake, exposure to common food allergens, observed allergic reactions or food sensitivities, and saved recipe preferences. 5. Infant Developmental Milestones Data: Reached developmental milestones and parent notes across Motor skills, Cognitive skills, Speech skills, and Social skills from age 0 to 24 months.

2.3. Information Collected Automatically

When you use the Services, we automatically collect limited technical information strictly required to maintain app functionality, security, and local scheduling: * Device & Technical Data: Device model, operating system type and version, App version, device language, crash logs, and diagnostic performance data. * Approximate Location & Time Zone: Your device’s time zone and country (derived from system settings or IP address) to accurately calculate daily routines, sleep schedules, and local reminders, and to display appropriate regional subscription pricing. We do not collect precise GPS location data. * In-App Usage & Local Preferences: Your saved favorite sleep soundscapes, sleep timer settings, and feature usage needed to generate your personal in-app statistics.

5. Data Retention and Account / Data Deletion

5.1. How Long We Retain Your Data

We retain your personal information and logged data for as long as your account remains active and as necessary to provide you with the Services. If you delete the App from your device without deleting your account, or if your account becomes inactive, we retain your encrypted data so you can restore your history if you reinstall the App. If your account remains completely inactive for twenty-four (24) consecutive months, we may delete your inactive account and associated data after sending a prior notice to your registered email address. You may request earlier deletion at any time.

5.2. How to Revoke Consent and Permanently Delete Your Account & Data

You have the absolute right to revoke your consent and delete your account and personal data at any time: 1. In-App Account Deletion (Direct & Self-Service): You can permanently delete your account and all associated TTC, Pregnancy, and Infant Care logs directly inside the App by navigating to Settings / Profile > Delete Account and confirming deletion. 2. Deletion via Email Request: You may also request complete account erasure, or deletion of specific data categories, at any time by emailing support@intervadasolutions.com from the email address associated with your account.

How Deletion Works & Timelines:
When you trigger account deletion in the App, your personal identifiers and health logs are immediately unlinked and purged from our active production database. If you submit a deletion request via email, we will process it within thirty (30) days (and within fifteen (15) business days where required by applicable U.S. state health privacy laws). Complete permanent erasure from encrypted disaster-recovery backup archives occurs automatically within thirty (30) to ninety (90) days. Please note that once deletion is completed, your logs cannot be recovered. We may retain only minimal, non-health transaction records where strictly required by tax, accounting, or statutory legal obligations.

Our Services are directed exclusively to adults (parents, expectant parents, and legal guardians) and are not intended for use by children. * Minimum Age Requirements: You must be at least 18 years of age to create an account and use the Services (or at least 16 years of age in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, and Canada, or 13 years of age in the United States and Australia with verifiable parent or guardian consent). * Infant and Child Care Logs: We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the EEA, UK, Switzerland, and Canada). Where you input information about your own baby or toddler into the App—such as your child’s birth date, sleep patterns, feeding history, known food allergens, or reached developmental milestones—that information is provided to us solely by you as the adult parent or legal guardian to assist you in tracking your child’s care. * Immediate Removal: If we discover or are notified that a child under the applicable minimum age has registered an account or submitted personal data directly to us without parental consent, we will immediately terminate the account and permanently delete all associated data. If you believe a child has provided data to us, please contact support@intervadasolutions.com.

7. Data Security

We implement rigorous technical, physical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect sensitive personal and health data against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure: * Encryption in Transit: All network traffic between the App/Website and our servers is encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (HTTPS / TLS 1.2 and TLS 1.3). * Encryption at Rest: All cloud database tables, authentication credentials, and backup snapshots are encrypted at rest using industry-standard AES-256 encryption. * Cryptographic Row-Level Security (RLS): Our database architecture enforces strict access controls so that your personal and family logs can only be queried by your authenticated user token (and any partner account you explicitly link). * Strict Internal Access Controls: Access to production infrastructure is restricted to authorized personnel bound by strict confidentiality obligations. * Security Incident Notification: While no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet is 100% impenetrable, in the unlikely event of a security breach affecting your personal data, we will promptly notify affected users and relevant data protection authorities in accordance with applicable law.
