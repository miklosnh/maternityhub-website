Privacy-First Maternal & Child Health

One Unified Journey from Conception to Toddlerhood

Maternity Hub seamlessly supports growing families across fertility tracking, pregnancy development, infant sleep schedules, first foods, and developmental milestones.

🌸

1. Trying to Conceive (TTC)

Comprehensive cycle intelligence and ovulation tracking designed with biological rhythm in mind.

  • Menstrual cycle & period logging
  • Basal Body Temperature (BBT) & Cervical Mucus
  • Fertile window & ovulation predictions
  • Symptom, hydration, and medication tracking
🌱

2. Pregnancy Care

Week-by-week fetal growth milestones, intuitive routine logs, and reassuring maternal wellness tracking.

  • Week-by-week fetal size & developmental facts
  • Fetal kick counter & movement logs
  • Maternal symptoms, appetite, and weight tracking
  • Hydration, activity, and rest monitoring
🌙

3. Sleep & Daily Care

Predictive sleep windows and daily care routines tailored to your baby's natural wake windows.

  • Algorithmic nap, wake window, and bedtime planning
  • Breastfeeding, bottle, and pumping logs
  • Diaper tracking, bath routines, and baby meds
  • Continuous background ambient loops & lullabies
🥑

4. First Foods & Solids

Guided introduction to solids, allergen tracking, and wholesome meal inspiration for growing babies.

  • Comprehensive food database across all groups
  • Common allergen exposure logging & reaction notes
  • Age-appropriate recipe ideas and textures
  • Pediatrician-informed safety principles
🧸

5. Milestones & Activities

Track your baby's motor, cognitive, speech, and social milestones from birth through 24 months.

  • Age-appropriate milestones (0 to 24 months)
  • Speech, cognitive, motor, and social skills
  • Play ideas and developmental activity suggestions
  • Informed by pediatric literature & Pathways.org
🛡️

6. Zero-Ad Privacy & Family Sync

Real-time family collaboration without sacrificing the privacy of your sensitive health data.

  • Zero data sale or commercial data brokerage
  • No third-party ad networks or tracking SDKs
  • Live real-time sync with partner or caregiver
  • End-to-end cloud encryption (AES-256 / TLS)

🔒 Your Data Stays Yours

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or health data. We never share your logs with advertising platforms or data brokers.

🩺 Educational & Supportive

Our algorithms provide gentle, mathematical routines and educational facts—never replacing professional medical or clinical advice.

👨‍👩‍👧 Family Collaboration

Seamlessly share daily care routines, feeding logs, and sleep timers with your spouse, partner, or trusted caregivers.
